Manual

Manual Trading

Manual trading is when you analyze the market, choose entries and exits, and manage risk in real time.

What It Demands

  • Time to analyze charts
  • Focus and discipline
  • Emotional control
Reality: Your results depend on how consistently you follow your rules.
Explore Manual Trading
Automation

Automated Trading

Automation is when a robot or system executes trades according to predefined rules and settings.

What You Must Know

  • It is NOT set and forget
  • Settings control risk
  • Good systems still require oversight
Warning: Wrong settings or unmanaged risk can multiply losses faster.
Explore Automated Trading
Key Difference

Manual = your mind executes the strategy.

Automation = the system executes the strategy.

In both, risk management is the real foundation.

The Reality

Why Traders Lose Money

Most traders are not lazy. Many simply operate without a clear process, defined risk or reliable feedback loop.

Common Trader Mistakes

  • Trading every signal with no data
  • Staking the same amount regardless of conditions
  • Adding money to losing positions
  • Switching systems every week
  • Trading without a daily plan

What Disciplined Traders Do Differently

  • Define daily risk and targets
  • Use systems based on rules
  • Trade setups they can explain
  • Track performance consistently
  • Review and improve their process
